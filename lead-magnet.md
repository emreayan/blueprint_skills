# Lead Magnet System

## Ne zaman
Email list growth, content marketing.

## Stack
Next.js + Resend + Vercel KV

## Süreç
1. Landing page (free PDF/template)
2. Email capture form
3. Email confirmation (double opt-in)
4. Otomatik download link
5. Welcome sequence
6. Tag (which magnet)
7. Conversion tracking

## Çıktı standardı
- Mobile form
- GDPR consent
- Double opt-in
- Magnet'a göre segment

## Yaygın hatalar
- Magnet hemen göster (email almadan)
- Welcome sequence yok
- Tagging eksik (segment imkansız)
